# ⚡THE-EV-KILOWATT-CHRONICLES
Fueling the transition towards intelligent transportation, this EV project synthesizes energy storage characteristics, propulsion performance, vehicle specifications, manufacturer positioning, ownership costs, and consumer perceptions into an interactive Power BI analytical experience.

**Built with a passion for electric vehicles and data analytics.**

🚀 Project Objectives

- Evaluate electric vehicle specifications across battery capacity, driving range, performance, and pricing.

- Examine sales distribution and manufacturer positioning across vehicle segments.

- Investigate relationships between battery capacity, driving range, vehicle performance, and affordability.

- Assess customer ratings to identify patterns in consumer preferences.

- Develop interactive Power BI dashboards that translate automotive data into meaningful business insights.

🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- Power BI — Interactive dashboards, data visualization, and performance reporting.

- Power Query — Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.

- DAX — Calculated measures, analytical KPIs, and dynamic reporting.

📊 Dashboard Modules

- **The Electric Frontier** (Electric Mobility Outlook) — Key performance indicators, manufacturer comparisons, pricing distribution, and market composition.

- **Range Beyond Limits** (EV Technology & Performance Pulse) — Comparative analysis of battery capacity, driving range, acceleration, top speed, and other vehicle specifications.

- **Beyond the Test Drive** (EV Customer Experience & Ownership) — Interactive brand-level exploration with model-specific comparisons across pricing, performance, and available customer ratings.

🔍 Key Analytical Areas

-  Battery capacity and driving-range comparisons.

-  Manufacturer-level and model-level performance benchmarking.

-  Pricing structures and vehicle affordability.

-  Sales distribution and manufacturer positioning.

-  Relationships between vehicle specifications and customer ratings.

-  Dynamic brand selection and model-level comparisons.

✨ Project Highlights

- Developed interactive Power BI dashboards for multidimensional electric vehicle analysis.

- Implemented data cleaning, transformation workflows using Power Query.

- Created KPI-driven visualizations and comparative performance metrics for automotive analysis.

- Enabled dynamic brand filtering and model-level exploration through interactive dashboard elements.
